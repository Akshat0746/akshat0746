## 💡 Fun Fact

> 🤖 Did you know? The term **"agent"** in Agentic AI comes from the idea of software that can **perceive, decide, and act autonomously** — much like a backend service that doesn't just respond to requests, but *anticipates* them. Good backend architecture and good AI agents follow the same principle: **clarity of purpose, minimal coupling, maximum reliability.**
